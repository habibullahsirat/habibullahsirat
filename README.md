## A little about me

I'm Habibullah Sirat, a Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate who enjoys turning ideas into useful software.

I work primarily on full-stack web development, with an interest in backend engineering, database systems, and modern application architecture.

Beyond the web, I enjoy exploring IoT, computer vision, and Edge AI — especially where software meets the physical world.

I believe in understanding the fundamentals, writing maintainable code, and continuously improving through practical development.
